Privacy Policy

What this policy covers

Last updated 2 October 2026.

EditsBeauty is a free browser photo editor from Jalangbam, near Brikama, The Gambia. This policy explains what happens to your photo and what the site itself uses.

Photos

We do not store uploaded photos. Editing happens on a canvas in your browser. Photos are deleted after the session. Closing the page or choosing another picture drops the one you were editing. Save writes a JPEG to your own device. That file is yours. EditsBeauty does not keep a copy on a server. The AI guide sends the words you type to Groq so it can choose the editor controls. It does not send the photo unless you turn on “Allow AI to see my photo (better results)” in Settings. That setting is off until you turn it on, and the first time you turn it on the page warns you. While it is on, asking the guide sends a small, compressed, low-resolution copy of the current photo with that request. EditsBeauty does not store that copy. Turning the setting off keeps the photo on this device.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies. Google AdSense may place cookies to show and measure ads. Those cookies come from Google, not from an EditsBeauty account. You can control ad cookies in your browser settings and in Google's ad settings. The editor does not require an account, and we do not ask you to sign in before you edit.

Ads may appear on the home page, About Us, this policy, and the terms. They are not placed on Settings, the contact form, the FAQ, or the short license notices. There is no ad unit next to Save, the camera button, or the contact form.

Diagnostic reports

Diagnostic reporting is off until you turn it on in Settings. Even then, nothing is collected or stored until you confirm a single report. A report may include the app version, the browser name and version, whether the device is mobile or desktop, an approximate screen size, the error message and stack trace, the tool or screen you were using, a short note you type, and the time you confirm. It does not include your photo, the canvas, face landmarks, your name, or your email. Confirming stores that list in the publisher’s database and opens an email to stisla2021@gmail.com with the same list. Turning the setting off stops diagnostic reporting completely. Photos and editing still stay on this device and are not uploaded.

Anonymous usage

EditsBeauty counts how the editor is used so it can be improved. A count may include a random id stored on this device, the day of the visit, whether the device is mobile or desktop, the browser name, an approximate country supplied by the host, which tools were opened, whether the AI guide was asked, whether “Allow AI to see my photo (better results)” was turned on, a rough session length, and a PWA install. An optional star rating can include a short note you type. That note is stored without your photo. Names, email addresses, photos, the canvas, and face data are not collected for these counts. The random id is not your name and is stored only as a short hash.

What stays on the device

  • Theme and other settings stay in this browser until you clear them.
  • A local cache can store the page so it opens again. Clear Cache in Settings removes those temporary files. It does not delete photos you already saved.
  • Face detection, when it runs, uses MediaPipe in this browser. It does not send your face to an EditsBeauty server.
