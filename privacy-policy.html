Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026.

EditsBeauty is a free browser photo editor from Serekunda, near Banjul, The Gambia. This policy explains what happens to your photo and what the site itself uses.

Photos

We do not store uploaded photos. Editing happens on a canvas in your browser. Photos are deleted after the session. Closing the page or choosing another picture drops the one you were editing. Save writes a JPEG to your own device. That file is yours. EditsBeauty does not keep a copy on a server.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies. Google AdSense may place cookies to show and measure ads. Those cookies come from Google, not from an EditsBeauty account. You can control ad cookies in your browser settings and in Google's ad settings. The editor does not require an account, and we do not ask you to sign in before you edit.

What stays on the device

  • Theme and other settings stay in this browser until you clear them.
  • A local cache can store the page so it opens again. Clear Cache in Settings removes those temporary files. It does not delete photos you already saved.
  • Face detection, when it runs, uses MediaPipe in this browser. It does not send your face to an EditsBeauty server.
