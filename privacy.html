Privacy Policy

EditsBeauty is an on-device photo editor. It is built to work without an account and without sending your pictures anywhere.

  • The app works offline after it has been installed or opened once.
  • We do not collect personal data, analytics, or advertising identifiers.
  • Photos you choose or capture stay on your device. They are drawn on a local canvas.
  • Edited photos are saved only when you tap Save. Nothing is uploaded to an EditsBeauty server.
  • Face detection uses MediaPipe in this browser. The model runs locally. It does not send your face to a server.
  • Settings and the app cache stay in this browser. Clear Cache removes temporary files and does not delete photos you already saved to your device.
