FAQ

Using the editor

Is EditsBeauty free?

Yes. The editor is 100% free. Filters, brightness, contrast, saturation, and Save do not ask for a subscription or an account.

Do I need to download an app?

No. EditsBeauty works in the browser on a phone or a computer. Start Editing opens a photo from your device. If you allow the camera, you can take the picture on this page. If the camera is blocked, choose a photo from your library instead.

How do I save the result?

Tap Save in the editor. The page writes a JPEG named editsbeauty-edit.jpg to your device. The file you started with is not overwritten.

Photos and privacy

Is my photo stored?

No. We do not store uploaded photos. The picture is drawn on a canvas in this browser and is deleted after the session. Closing the page drops it. A saved JPEG stays on your device, not on an EditsBeauty server. Anonymous usage counts do not include the photo, your name, or your email.

Who can see the picture while I edit?

The edit runs on your device. Face detection, when it runs, uses MediaPipe in this browser. It does not send your face to an EditsBeauty server. The AI guide sees the photo only if you turn on “Allow AI to see my photo (better results)” in Settings and then ask it. That copy is small, temporary, and not stored. Read the Privacy Policy for cookies and ads.

The site

Who publishes EditsBeauty?

StISLA2021 publishes EditsBeauty from Jalangbam, near Brikama, The Gambia. Ownership and the longer description of each filter are on About Us.

Where do ads appear?

Google AdSense may show ads on pages with reading content. Ads are not placed on this FAQ, on Settings, on the contact form, or on the short license notices. There is no ad button next to Save or the camera control.

How do I report a problem?

Email stisla2021@gmail.com or use Contact Us. The form opens your email app and does not store the message. Diagnostic reports stay off until you turn them on in Settings. After you confirm, that short list can be stored in a database and emailed to the publisher. It never includes your photo, the canvas, or face landmarks.
