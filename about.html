About EditsBeauty

EditsBeauty is a free photo editor for beauty retouching. It runs in the browser, so there is no app to install. The team builds it in Jalangbam, near Brikama, in The Gambia.

The mission is to make beauty editing free for everyone. A selfie, a portrait, or an Instagram photo should be editable without a subscription and without sending the picture to a server.

Free beauty photo editor

EditsBeauty is a free online photo editor for beauty retouching. Smooth skin, remove acne, fix double chin, add lipstick and eyelashes, change background with AI. No app download needed. Upload your photo and edit in 5 seconds. Perfect for selfies, portraits, and Instagram.

The background tool keeps the person and places a real scene photo behind them. Beach, city, studio, garden, sunset, and the other scenes are pictures, not a generated image. The cutout runs on this device. The photo is not sent to a server for the background. The guide reads the words you type, and looks at a small copy of the photo only after you turn that on in Settings. It runs smooth, a look, Adjust, crop, or a named background with the tools already on the page.

A portrait used to wait on someone else. You sent the file, you waited, and you hoped the retouch still looked like you. EditsBeauty keeps that work on the page you already opened. The picture is drawn on a canvas in your browser. Filters, brightness, and skin tools update the same picture you are looking at. You do not install a store app, and you do not make an account.

People use it for the photos they already take. A selfie before a message. A portrait for a profile. A picture for Instagram that needs cleaner skin or a calmer background. The tools are named for those jobs. Smooth Skin softens texture. Acne quiets spots. Double Chin eases the jaw. Lipstick and Eyelashes add the small face details that photographs often miss. AI BG Change keeps you and swaps the scene behind you. Red Car Light and Golden Hour change the color of the light.

The editor is meant to be quick. Open the site, tap Start Editing, and choose a photo from your camera roll. If you allow the camera, you can take the picture here. A denied camera does not block you. The page asks for a photo instead. Once the image is on the canvas, every filter button paints a visible change. Adjust holds Auto, Exposure, Brilliance, Highlights, Shadows, Contrast, Brightness, Black Point, Saturation, Vibrance, Warmth, Tint, Sharpness, Definition, Noise Reduction, and Vignette. Each slider updates the picture while your finger is still on the control. Save writes a JPEG named editsbeauty-edit.jpg. The file lands in your downloads. The original on your device stays where it was.

EditsBeauty is free for everyone. There is no trial week and no locked filter. Students, shops, and families can use the same page. It runs on a phone browser and on a desktop browser. Face tools run on the device. Photos are not uploaded to an EditsBeauty server, and they are not kept after you leave the session. Advertising on the site may use cookies, which is explained in the privacy policy.

Use one photo at a time. Pick one filter and look at the canvas before you stack another. If the first look is too strong, choose Original in the filter row or move brightness back toward the middle. The picture on the canvas is the picture you will save. That is the whole product: a browser, a photo, and a result you can post.

Brightness is the control people reach for first. Drag it and the canvas updates immediately, because the slider writes a CSS filter onto the drawing and paints again on every change. Contrast and saturation sit beside it and work the same way. Film, Warm, Glow, Cool, and the other looks in the filter row are the same canvas path. Nothing waits on a server round trip. If you are on a slow connection, the page you already loaded can still edit the photo in front of you.

How It Works

  1. Upload Photo. Tap Start Editing, a Face Details card, or the camera button, then choose a picture from your device.
  2. Choose Filter. Pick Smooth, Acne, Double Chin, Lipstick, Eyelashes, AI BG Change, Red Car Light, or Golden Hour. Each one paints on the canvas.
  3. Download. Tap Save. The edited JPEG downloads to your device.

Face Details Explained

SMOOTH SKIN. Softens skin, removes texture, keeps natural look. A 0.8 pixel blur and a 105% brightness lift even the face without a heavy mask.

ACNE. Softens the skin so spots and rough patches look quieter. The blur stays on the face so the rest of the photo keeps its detail.

DOUBLE CHIN. Gently lifts the area under the jaw. The change is a small reshape on the canvas, not a new face.

LIPSTICK. Adds a red tint on the mouth. Color sits on the lips so cheeks, eyes, and background stay as they were.

EYELASHES. Darkens the lash line on both eyes. The bands are short so brows and skin keep their shape.

AI BG Change. Blurs what is behind the person. The face stays sharper than the room, which is the usual portrait separation.

RED CAR LIGHT. Lays a red glow on one side and a cooler tone on the other. It reads like light from a car at night.

GOLDEN HOUR. Warms the whole frame with a soft amber wash. Skin looks sunlit without taking a new photo.
