About EditsBeauty

EditsBeauty is a free photo editor for beauty retouching. It runs in the browser, so there is no app to install. The team builds it in Serekunda, near Banjul, in The Gambia.

The mission is to make beauty editing free for everyone. A selfie, a portrait, or an Instagram photo should be editable without a subscription and without sending the picture to a server.
