How to reach us

EditsBeauty is published by StISLA2021 in Jalangbam, near Banjul, The Gambia. Write to stisla2021@gmail.com about the editor, a privacy request, or a correction on this site.

There is no support phone line and no office counter. Email is the working contact. A reply comes from that same address when we can answer.

What to include

  • For a bug, name the button you tapped and the phone or browser you used.
  • For a privacy request, say what you want removed or explained. Photos edited in the browser are not kept on a server.
  • Do not attach a photo of someone else unless you have the right to send it.

Send a message

The form opens your email app with the message filled in. It does not store the text on this website. If no email app opens, copy the address above and send the message yourself.
